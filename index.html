          Month reports

          After each payday, fill the month in on the Quest screen and press Done. Each month has a report: what you beat, what grew and what you earned.

          Bank spending

          Import a statement from Bank of Scotland: the PDF from the banking app, or a CSV from online banking. It is read on this phone only, and nothing is sent anywhere.

          Backup

          Your figures are saved in this browser on this phone only. Clearing your browser data would erase every month, so copy a backup now and then and keep it in a note.

          Practice data is a made-up year to play with. It replaces everything, so copy a backup first and restore it when you’re done.
